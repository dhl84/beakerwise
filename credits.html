BeakerWise credits

This page names the work of others in BeakerWise (BeakerWise: The Real Science Lab): the files, the data, the curriculum text, the software and the voice model. It also names our own work. It is free to read, with no login.

  • These third-party files keep their own licences. The store terms do not limit your rights under the licences of the third-party material listed here.
  • The developer and publisher is dav3studios.
  • The app names no exam board. Where a fact comes from an exam board's practical handbook, the app names the source "School practical handbooks". No exam board made, checked or approved BeakerWise.

The voice

Every spoken line is a recording that we made with a computer voice: the Kokoro-82M text-to-speech model (hexgrad, Apache License 2.0, https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro/blob/main/LICENSE), voice bm_george. No person recorded the lines. The model itself is not in the app.

Sources of the facts

The measured values in the app come from source pages, and the "How we know" card of each experiment names its sources. Our checking tool found a quote for each of these facts on its source page. Not every number is measured: 93 of the 104 experiments also use estimates or values chosen for the activity (for example the power of a warm plate). The app marks them: the label on each experiment says when some values are estimates, and the "How we know" card lists each estimate. We name these bodies and authors as sources only.
